# Paper 7 — local AI content business research brief

## Authorized outcome
A rigorous, evidence-linked business plan and layered pseudo-instance for social-media content generated locally, with high automation and no meetings/calls. This is research/design first: no public posting, account creation, purchases, model downloads, new production implementation or income claims.

## Working business hypothesis, open to rejection
A mostly automated channel can repeatedly create original, useful educational content whose eventual net receipts exceed cash costs and valued operator time. Ad-supported video is a candidate, not a proven winner. Compare affiliates and self-service products. Ads produce zero receipts before eligibility/acceptance; eligibility is not guaranteed.

## Meaning of local
Intended script generation, narration, optional asset generation, visual/code animation and rendering occur on the user's Mac. Research/data access, distribution, analytics and payments inherently involve outside services. The research fleet itself uses hosted assistants; it is not evidence that production inference is already local. Distinguish AI-generated pixels from original software demonstrations or AI-authored code rendered locally. All-local generation does not mean offline business operations or no platform dependence.

## User constraints and unresolved choices
- Social-media business; no calls, meetings or appearing on camera required.
- Highest practical automation subject to originality, quality, rights, platform acceptance and positive economics.
- Local Apple Silicon machine; current hardware/runtime evidence to be independently inventoried.
- No guaranteed zero human contact: written support, disputes or verification may be necessary.
- Country/legal account basis, language, existing audience/channel eligibility, budget, time cap, niche expertise and approved voice/assets remain unknown. Do not infer residence from timezone or equate public availability with commercial reuse rights.
- Hardware is already owned; report both marginal cash cost and owner-time/economic cost. No fabricated CPM/RPM, views, conversion rates or production benchmarks.

## Research roles and evidence classes
Platform/policy track, local-production/license track, audience/business track; coordinator synthesizes economics and layer contracts. Primary-source facts, current local observations, mathematical implications, authored assumptions and validation targets are distinct evidence classes. A citation proves the cited source's claim, not market demand or business success.

## Definition of a useful delivery
A recommended conditional starting model/niche/format with defensible alternatives; explicit business/revenue path; production and intelligence architecture; cost/threshold sensitivity model; stage-gated validation and falsification; rights/provenance requirements; named unresolved decisions; and a pseudo-instance decomposed top-down then grounded bottom-up against actual available native/library/local capabilities. Missing capabilities stay gaps. A polished paper is not a validated business.
