The representative audit is selected by the existing exploratory ranking, not a fresh random sample or held-out test. The 445 groups are dependent and may originate from copied designs. ### Study C limitations

The split is retrospective; the source corpus was already explored. Group hashing creates unequal numbers of graphs and neighborhoods, copied/modified designs may cross undetected, and no population-level inference follows. Native random seeds were not controlled; repeated20-second runs are stochastic observations. The accepted feature rules serialize associations; their utility does not establish richer topology learning. Masked saved-edge features measure source-pattern recovery, not behavior, necessity or the only valid design. Proposal descriptors are chosen from saved training declarations, can pool differing implementations under a type label, and are not certified native Langflow contracts. Fresh symbolic neighbors leave aliasing unresolved; acyclicity is a construction property, not a learned guarantee. Memory failure is infrastructural. No experiment executes a workflow, evaluates a user task or creates a higher plane.

### Study E: structural construction is not task realization

Study E creates proposed upper-plane records, not validated operational composites, flows or processes. One authored tower and one prompt-proposal revision cannot establish general decomposition quality or autonomous construction. The normalized schema and checker were co-developed; assertion counts and fifteen detected mutations are developmental coverage, not independent samples or comprehensive safety proof. Repairs addressed concrete structural false positives without resolving task semantics.

An important unresolved research gap is **task-grounded realization adequacy**: does this boundary and information path support a useful critique under an approved triage policy? The review Agent receives draft.response, not a guaranteed original-ticket witness. Retaining that gap passes an honesty invariant but does not solve the task. Prompt wording cannot prove preservation of the original source. This gap requires task criteria and behavior evidence, not simply more structural assertions.

Cut equality covers actual wires, not every unwired public interface or resource dependency. Requirement-to-gate propagation is also not a complete semantic calculus: process-owned policy/behavior requirements may constrain lower capabilities even when not repeated in child obligation lists. No child should be called ready on that omission. Native offline type resolution can use defaults; dynamic fields may require server-side updates. The source report and official documentation cross-check distinguish such metadata from active ports and note prospective subflow/human-approval limitations. No subflow execution or approval integration was tested.

All ten explicit obligations remain open. Model bindings, approved prompts/policy, human accountability, coordination, source access, behavior and retention are not supplied by native assembly. Native machinery reuse therefore validates neither the proposed ontology nor useful generation, and cannot rescue Study C's negative comparison.
