# Stock builder collaboration synthesis

## Three-role cycle
1. Coordinator freezes the plain request, local implementation/prompt/toolkit provenance, private initial state and run-specific evidence. The generation subject alone chooses graph/configuration through its normal API; it does not receive our layer recipe.
2. Research collaborator interprets frozen outputs using native identities, explicit requirements and alternative proposed boundaries. Configuration, Composite, Flows and Processes interpretations are separate authored documents; they are not subject-generated planes or behavioral findings.
3. Coordinator/checker tests graph fidelity, cuts, configuration ownership, obligation inheritance and evidence tiers against original artifacts. Research collaborator corrects interpretation mistakes in new versions. Only an actual unmet frozen requirement or authorized clarification justifies a task-level follow-up to the unchanged subject. Freeze each subsequent outcome separately; never erase the first.

These are distinct internal roles, not external independent evaluation. Source stability during observation does not mean pristine upstream. Neither graph construction nor pipeline success establishes task behavior.

## Task revision decision and remaining uncertainty
No specific contradiction or omission of the original build request is established at the available structural/instruction tier: input, category/urgency/reply instructions, separate critique stage, human-consideration presentation, no-action instructions and explicit provisional assumptions are present. Behavior is untested and first-run no-execution remains incompletely observable. That uncertainty must be recorded, not relabeled task success or necessarily repaired with another generation. No further subject generation/task execution is recommended now.

Do not demand independent original-source transport or enforceable human approval as if those were original requirements. They are defensible proposed follow-ups, conditional on user authorization. Ask whether model-carried evidence is acceptable for the intended review and whether consideration alone or recorded human approval is needed. If the user adopts the stronger source criterion, request preserved verifiable ticket evidence in task language, not components/edges. If approved organizational triage is desired, obtain taxonomy/SLA/escalation and retention decisions rather than invent them. Without that authorization, stop with the frozen first outcome and proposed interpretations.

The scientific gap is still whether the selected roles, boundary and information path yield faithful useful critique. Stock request-to-graph selection is now observed; automatic upper-plane discovery, runtime correctness, task safety, generalization and incremental mining benefit remain unestablished. This turn produced an interpretation-only revision and synthesis, not graph/source changes, execution or manuscript edits.
