Build a character voice.

Start with a voice you like. Carry that voice through performances, listening checks and a reusable casting profile.

Voice design and new-line checks use VoxCPM2; acted-reference performance transfer uses IndexTTS 2.5. Both run locally. Design directions guide the result; they do not guarantee age, accent or acting. Word checks support listening, and never approve a performance automatically. Your earlier recordings remain in the audition archive.
