Design a voice.

Describe a speaker, generate a short audition, then save the version you like. This page uses VoxCPM2 locally to create a new voice without an example recording.

Describe your voice

Describe age range, vocal presentation, pitch, accent and texture in words. These guide the model; they are not exact age or pitch controls.

Generation settings
The same recipe and seed help repeat an experiment. They do not guarantee identical sound across software or devices.

Saved voice profiles

A profile keeps one selected recording as the voice reference. “Try current script” speaks new words from that saved sample, without applying changes to the description.

Auditions

Newest first. Play replays a saved recording; generating creates a new one. Listen before naming and saving a voice.

Voice design is not editing an existing recording. Saved profiles can be reused here; they are not yet connected to scene casting. No character chat is included. Engine source and documentation
